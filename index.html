FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call us at (212) 204-8589.

How do I book a car or limousine in New York City?

Reserve online through our secure booking page at any hour: choose your service, pickup and drop-off, select a vehicle and confirm with payment. You receive a confirmation by email. You can also call (212) 204-8589.

Which airports do you serve?

We provide chauffeured transfers to and from JFK, LaGuardia (LGA), Newark Liberty (EWR), Teterboro (TEB) for private aviation, and Westchester County Airport (HPN), as well as other regional airports on request.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Include your airline and flight number when booking. We monitor your flight and adjust the pickup time to your actual arrival so your chauffeur is waiting when you land.

Do you offer hourly or as-directed service?

Yes. Book a car and chauffeur by the hour for meetings, roadshows, shopping, nights out or multi-stop itineraries anywhere in the New York metropolitan area.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Reservations and payments are processed through our encrypted online booking platform. Your rate is shown before you confirm.

How far in advance should I reserve?

We recommend reserving as early as possible, especially for weddings, holidays and peak travel dates. For same-day or last-minute requests, call us and we will do our best to accommodate you.
